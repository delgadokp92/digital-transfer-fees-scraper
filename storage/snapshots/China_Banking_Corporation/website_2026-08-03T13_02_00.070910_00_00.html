My CBC Business set to transform corporate digital banking

Chinabank has rolled out its next-generation corporate digital banking facility, My CBC Business, introducing a vastly improved level of operational efficiency for large corporations as well as small and medium enterprises.

My CBC Business serves as a secure, one-stop business banking solution that combines advanced cash management, payment processing, and real-time account monitoring into a unified digital ecosystem. The new platform bridges the gap between banking and business operations by enabling real-time host-to-host system integration, automated transaction processing, fortified security controls, and accelerated client onboarding capabilities.

“Our goal is to make digital banking frictionless for our clients and help them manage the financial side of their operations more efficiently so they can focus on scaling their businesses,” said Cash Management Services & Operations Group Head Domingo Dayro, Jr., noting that the upgrade was shaped by client feedback and evolving business needs.

The new platform is powered by technology developed in partnership with Solutions Exchange Inc.  It offers a complete suite of features, including:

  • Liquidity & Cash Flows: End-to-end receivables and payables management, auto-debit/credit arrangements, automated enterprise payroll processing, bills payment, and strategic liquidity optimization (Sure Sweep).
  • Treasury & Transfers: On-demand foreign currency trading and instantaneous domestic fund transfers routed via InstaPay and PESONet.
  • Check Services: Self-service and outsourced check issuance, post-dated check warehousing, checkbook reordering, and digital stop payment orders.

To ensure continuous operational mobility, users can manage their corporate assets via a true omni-channel experience—either through a standard desktop web browser or via the My CBC Business mobile app, now available on the Apple App Store and Google Play.

My CBC Business reflects Chinabank’s commitment to enhancing its digital banking services for corporate clients. It replaces Chinabank Online Corporate, which will be decommissioned once all products have been migrated to the new platform and all clients have completed the transition.
