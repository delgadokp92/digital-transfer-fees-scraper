Jesus Antonio S. Itchon

Jesus Antonio S. Itchon is retiring as president of the Sy family's BDO Network Bank after more than nine years at the helm, having overseen its transformation from a rural lender into the country's fourth-largest thrift bank by assets.

Itchon will step down effective Jan. 1, 2027, but will remain involved in the business as vice chair and non-executive director.

BDO Unibank Inc. announced on Friday that Alberto Osorio Quiogue, currently BDO Network Bank's chief operating officer, will succeed Itchon.

Countryside expansion

Formerly known as One Network Bank, the Mindanao-focused lender was acquired by BDO from the Consunji family through a P6.7-billion share-swap transaction in 2015, extending the banking giant's reach into underserved communities.

From just 98 active branches at the time of the acquisition, the lender expanded to 545 branches and 92 loan offices nationwide by the end of 2025.

It began operating as a thrift bank in May 2025 and had amassed P179 billion in assets and a P149.2-billion gross loan portfolio by the end of September 2026.

Leadership succession

Quiogue joined the BDO Group in 2016 and previously headed BDO Network Bank's salary loan and small business lending operations before becoming chief operating officer in January 2026.

The banking veteran, whose career includes stints at the Development Bank of the Philippines, Standard Chartered Bank in Dubai and Security Bank, will assume the presidency subject to confirmation by the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas' Monetary Board.
