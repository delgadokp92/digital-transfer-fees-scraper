Insider Spotlight

  • UnionBank is expanding its digital payments ecosystem by enabling Apple Pay for cardholders in the Philippines
  • The move reflects the bank's strategy to simplify everyday transactions while strengthening payment security
  • Customers can use eligible UnionBank cards with iPhone, Apple Watch, iPad, and Mac for contactless, in-app, and online purchases



UnionBank is reinforcing its digital banking strategy by making mobile payments more seamless and secure, as financial institutions continue to compete on convenience and user experience rather than traditional banking services alone.

Why it matters

The bank on Tuesday said its cardholders in the Philippines can now use Apple Pay, allowing them to make contactless purchases in stores, online, and within apps using supported Apple devices. The move expands payment options for customers as digital wallets and contactless transactions become increasingly common.

The rationale

Rather than introducing a new banking product, the initiative aligns with UnionBank's broader effort to integrate widely used digital platforms into its ecosystem. The bank said customers can authenticate payments through Face ID, Touch ID, or a device passcode, with each transaction secured by a one-time dynamic security code. 

Actual card numbers are not stored on the device or Apple servers, with payment credentials instead protected through a unique Device Account Number stored in the Secure Element.

What they're saying

“As we continue building a next-generation bank, we're focused on making everyday banking and payments simpler, safer, and more intuitive. Bringing Apple Pay to UnionBank cardholders enhances the payment experience by allowing customers to pay quickly and securely with the devices they use every day. Whether they're shopping in-store, online, or in-app, customers can enjoy a more seamless way to pay, with the security and convenience they expect from UnionBank,” Union Bank of the Philippines president and CEO Ana Aboitiz Delgado said in a press statement.

How it works

Customers can add eligible UnionBank cards through the Wallet app on iPhone and use Apple Pay immediately across iPhone, Apple Watch, iPad, and Mac. The bank said cardholders will continue to receive the rewards and benefits associated with their UnionBank cards when using the service. Apple Pay can also be used for faster online and in-app purchases without repeatedly entering card or shipping information.

Bottom line

The integration underscores how Philippine banks are increasingly competing through digital payment capabilities, with customer convenience, security, and ecosystem partnerships becoming key differentiators in retail banking. —Vanessa Hidalgo | Ed: Corrie S. Narisma
