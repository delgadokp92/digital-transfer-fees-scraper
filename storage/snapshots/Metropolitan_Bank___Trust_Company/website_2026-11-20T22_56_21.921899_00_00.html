Insider Spotlight

  • Metrobank is expanding its financial education initiatives through artificial intelligence to help Filipinos make more informed money decisions
  • Its new AI-powered chatbot, Ask Earnest, allows users to explore everyday financial questions, from budgeting and saving to debt management and investing
  • The initiative builds on Metrobank's Earnest financial education platform, which offers digital content and community-based learning programs



Metrobank is strengthening its digital financial education strategy as more Filipinos turn to artificial intelligence (AI) for information and guidance on managing their money.

The bank has introduced Ask Earnest, an AI-powered financial mindfulness chatbot designed to help users understand financial concepts and make more informed decisions about their personal finances.

Money remains a sensitive topic for many Filipinos, making it difficult for some to seek financial guidance. Metrobank aims to address this hesitation by providing a conversational platform where users can raise questions without feeling uncomfortable.

Unlike traditional financial education materials, the chatbot allows users to ask follow-up questions and explore financial topics at their own pace.

Practical financial concerns 

"Financial education should meet people where they are. We want it to be something people can turn to when they have a question or a decision to make. Sometimes, the first hurdle is knowing where to begin or feeling comfortable enough to ask," Metrobank chief marketing officer Digs Dimagiba said in a press statement.

Ask Earnest covers practical financial concerns, including building emergency funds, managing debt, evaluating purchases, and understanding investment options.

Users can also seek explanations of financial terminology or explore considerations before lending money to friends or relatives.

The platform is intended to encourage users to understand their options before making financial commitments.

Financial literacy advocacy

Ask Earnest forms part of Metrobank's broader financial literacy advocacy under its Earnest platform.

Originally introduced as an e-book offering personal finance lessons, Earnest subsequently expanded into a website and social media channels covering money management, savings, and financial protection.

Metrobank also developed the Moneygurado docuseries, which examined how Filipino culture, history, and personal experiences influence financial behavior.

The bank complemented these digital initiatives with financial education roadshows involving schools and organizations.

Filipinos can access Ask Earnest through earnest.ph, where the chatbot is available on the website. —Vanessa Hidalgo | Ed: Corrie S. Narisma
