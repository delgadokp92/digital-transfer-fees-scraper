Insider Spotlight

  • Metrobank strengthens its wealth management position by combining investment expertise with personalized client service
  • The bank secured two Philippine distinctions at the Citywire ASEAN Awards 2025/26
  • Metrobank Trust gains further international recognition for investment performance, reliability, and client-focused financial solutions



Metrobank is reinforcing its position in the Philippine wealth management market by combining investment expertise, diversified financial solutions, and personalized client service to help investors navigate changing market conditions.

The bank secured two distinctions at the Citywire ASEAN Awards 2025/26, winning Best Asset Manager and Best Client Services in the Philippines.

Why it matters

The recognitions highlight the growing importance of integrating investment performance with responsive client support as financial institutions compete for long-term wealth management relationships.

Metrobank's trust banking group serves clients pursuing financial goals ranging from wealth accumulation to future financial security, supported by investment research, portfolio management, and client services.

“Clients entrust us with money they have worked hard to build, and with goals that matter to them and their families. Our responsibility is to bring sound investment management and attentive service to every stage of that relationship,” said Ric Pedrosa, Metrobank trust banking group head.

Driving client value

Metrobank is focusing on expanding access to investment solutions while helping clients make informed financial decisions amid evolving economic and market conditions.

“These recognitions encourage us to keep improving how we serve our clients, from broadening access to relevant investment solutions to helping them make informed decisions as their needs and market conditions evolve,” Pedrosa added.

Behind the recognition

The Citywire ASEAN Awards evaluate financial institutions across Malaysia, the Philippines, and Thailand based on innovation, business growth, and commitment to client service, according to a company release.

Assessment criteria include growth in assets under management and administration, product diversity, distribution capabilities, technology enhancements, and market differentiation.

The bigger picture

Metrobank's latest distinctions add to its growing portfolio of industry recognitions.

The bank ranked first among Philippine banks and securities firms in the Local Currency Bonds and G3 Bonds categories of The Asset Benchmark Research Awards 2026.

Its Metro$ Short Term Bond Fund also earned Best Managed Fund recognition from CFA Society Philippines.

Meanwhile, Euromoney named Metrobank the Philippines' Best Bank for 2026, while the Asian Banker recognized it as the country's Strongest Bank for the fifth consecutive year in 2025.

What's next

The dual recognition reinforces Metrobank's strategy of pairing investment management capabilities with client-focused services, positioning its trust banking business to respond to changing investor needs and sustain long-term relationships in an increasingly competitive wealth management market. —Princess Daisy C.
