Things to Know

  • Must be at least 18 years old
  • Has any of the following government IDs:
    • Philippine Passport
    • Driver’s License
    • Philippine National ID
    • Unified Multi-purpose ID (UMID)
    • Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) ID
  • With active e-mail address and mobile number
  • No minimum balance required

ACCOUNT OPENING

  1. Can I open an eSavings account anytime?
    • You can open an account online daily from 4:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m.
  2. Will I still need to go to a branch to complete my Metrobank eSavings account opening?
    • No need. You can complete the savings account online application through the Metrobank App or Earnest Investment App. You will immediately receive your semi-verified Metrobank eSavings account details via email.
  3. What type of smartphone can I use to open Metrobank eSavings account?
    • For eSavings account opening, you can use any Android phone or iPhone that meets the following requirements: 
      • Android: Minimum of Android 8.0 (Oreo)
      • iOS: Minimum of iOS 12
  4.  How long will it take to open my Metrobank eSavings account? 
    • The process takes around 10 minutes. If there are no issues with your application, you will receive a semi-verified account immediately.
    • A semi-verified account has an incoming deposit limit and outstanding balance limit of PHP 50,000.
    • A fully verified account has no incoming deposit limit and outstanding balance limit
  5. I don’t want to use my own mobile number to open an account. Can I use my relative’s or friend’s number instead?
    • No. For your account’s security, we strongly recommend using your own personal mobile number.
  6. How will I know if I was successful in opening the Metrobank eSavings account? 
    • You will see an on-screen confirmation that your eSavings account has been created, and you will receive an email from Metrobank containing your complete account details.
  7. I lost the original copy of my valid ID. Can I use a scanned copy instead?
    • No. A scanned copy will not be accepted for online account opening.
  8. Is it possible to open a Joint OR/AND eSavings account?
    • No. Metrobank eSavings accounts are for individual accounts only.
  9. Can I open multiple Metrobank eSavings accounts?
    • No. You can only open one Metrobank eSavings account.
  10. I have an existing Metrobank Savings/Checking/Payroll account. Can I still open a Metrobank eSavings account?
    • No, but you can still open a Metrobank savings account at any Metrobank branch. We are working to offer this facility in the future.

BALANCE AND TRANSACTION LIMITS

  1. How can I deposit money into my Metrobank eSavings account?  
    • You can fund your account through the following channels: 
      • Intrabank Fund Transfer
      • Instapay
      • PESONet
      • Cash Accept Machine (CAM) or Cash Recycler Machine (CRM)
      • Partner Pawnshops (Remit to Account)
      • Remittance
  2. What happens if I deposit more than PHP 50,000 to my semi-verified account? 
    • If you exceed the monthly deposit limit of your semi-verified account, the incoming/deposit transaction will automatically be rejected. 
  3. Can I request to increase my incoming/deposit transaction limit? 
    • For semi-verified accounts, increase of limits is not allowed. 
    • Fully verified accounts do not have incoming deposit transaction limits. 
  4. Can I make outgoing financial transactions in the Metrobank Online/Mobile App immediately after account enrollment?  
    • No. There is a 24-hour cooling period before you can perform outgoing financial transactions (e.g., send/move money, pay bills, send load) through the Metrobank Online or Mobile App.  

ACCOUNT SERVICING

  1. Can I request for a debit card? 
    • Yes, debit cards are available for fully verified Metrobank eSavings accounts.
    • Email our customer support at onlineproductsupport@metrobank.com.ph and claim your debit card at your preferred branch.
    • Please include the following in your email:
      • Complete Name
      • Last 4 digits of your account number
      • Preferred branch for card pickup
      • Date of pick-up
    • The first debit card is free. A replacement card will be subject to fee. 
  2. What if I visit the branch without an appointment? Will they still accommodate me? 
    • Yes, walk-in requests will still be accommodated.
  3. Can I withdraw funds without a debit card? 
    • Yes. You can perform cardless withdrawals at any Metrobank or PSBank ATM via the Metrobank Mobile App or Metrobank Online (choose "Cardless Withdrawal").
    • You can also do over-the-counter transactions at any Metrobank branch. An OTC fee applies. 
  4. Can I request a Bank Certificate using my Metrobank eSavings account? 
    • Yes. Please send your request to onlineproductsupport@metrobank.com.ph.
    • The certificate will be available for pick-up:
      • Metro Manila: 3–5 banking days
      • Countryside: 5–7 banking days
  5. Where can I send my inquiries or concerns about my Metrobank eSavings account? 
    • Email us at onlineproductsupport@metrobank.com.ph
    • Call our customer hotline at (02) 88-700-700
    • You can also reach our Customer Care Team at customercare@metrobank.com.ph
