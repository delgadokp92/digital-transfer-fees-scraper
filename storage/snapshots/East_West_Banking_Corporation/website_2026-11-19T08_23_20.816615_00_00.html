I. Introduction to EasyWay Mobile App

EasyWay is an upgraded online and mobile banking platform designed to deliver advanced digital products and an exceptional user experience, aligned with EastWest's commitment to make banking fast, easy and convenient. 

With EasyWay, you can access the following banking services: 

  • Bills Payment 
  • Funds transfers to other EastWest / EastWest Rural Bank / Komo accounts 
  • Sending money to local banks via InstaPay and PESONet 
  • Opening a Peso or Dollar Time Deposit account 
  • Applying for an EastWest Credit Card 
  • Opening additional Foreign or Peso Savings accounts 
  • For linked credit cards, users can a) enroll Quick Bills merchants to their account, b) convert new credit card retail or straight charge transaction into installments., and/or c) transfer credit card balance or convert card balance to cash 

 

EasyWay also lets users access and manage their accounts securely, with features like:  

  • Biometrics log in and authentication 
  • Debit / Prepaid Card settings management 
  • Transaction notification management 
  • Account customization e.g., customizing names for deposit accounts, or toggling visibility of accounts as needed 
  • In-app communication with Customer Service 

 

All these and more still coming soon on EasyWay!
