Insider Spotlight

  • RCBC encourages households to maximize idle funds as living expenses remain elevated
  • Hexagon Club extends banking privileges to up to four household members
  • Members can access insurance, telemedicine and higher-yield savings options



Rizal Commercial Banking Corp. (RCBC) is urging Filipino households to make their savings work harder as elevated food, fuel and electricity costs continue to squeeze family budgets.

The bank is encouraging depositors to rethink how they manage everyday expenses, protect emergency funds and maximize returns on savings amid persistent pressure on purchasing power.

Why it matters

Volatile oil prices continue to affect transportation and electricity expenses, while weather-related supply disruptions have pushed up the cost of essential food items, including rice and vegetables.

RCBC said in a company release that households can better manage rising living costs by cutting unnecessary banking fees and putting idle savings into higher-yield financial products.

“This is a good time to review how your money is working for you. Small, practical adjustments like putting idle cash to work, managing spending more strategically, and making the most of the benefits available to you can make a real difference over time,” RCBC head of retail banking group Richard C. Lim said.

The bigger picture

RCBC is positioning its Hexagon Club premium banking program as a financial management platform that helps families manage expenses, safeguard funds and build savings.

The program waives common banking fees and provides priority branch services, while its Hexagon Club Family tier allows primary members to extend privileges to up to four household members.

Richard C. Lim 
RCBC head of retail banking group

More than savings

Eligible Hexagon Club Privilege and Priority members receive complimentary life insurance coverage, while Hexagon Club Family subscribers and supplementary members can access free telemedicine services.

Members receiving overseas remittances also benefit from preferential foreign exchange rates, helping cushion the impact of currency fluctuations.

For households seeking better returns, Hexagon Club offers access to time deposits and money market instruments while keeping everyday transaction funds accessible.

What to know

Membership requires a minimum average balance of P100,000 in an eligible RCBC peso or foreign currency deposit account. —Princess Daisy C. Ominga | Ed: Corrie S. Narisma
